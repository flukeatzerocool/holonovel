## What went wrong / rework

1. **Baseline premise was inherited, not measured** (plan v1 claimed a green
   `check:fast`; it was copied from the previous plan). Measured at execution
   start: `40 passed, 1 failed` — the failure being this increment's own missing
   `aar.md`. Corrected as amendment A1 and re-stamped before the first edit.
   Cost: one re-stamp. Lesson: run the baseline before writing the plan.
2. **First `build-order` failed on `check-registers:strict`** — a pre-existing
   failure: the previous increment left F-P1-7/8/9 (resolved) under
   `## Scheduled-roadmap`, and F-S1 cited a REQ the roadmap no longer names.
   Fixed within this increment (rows moved to a dated `Resolved` section; the
   stale parenthetical dropped) because the register is in the blast radius.
   Verified pre-existing by inspection of the committed file.
3. **Pattern-buffer ordering** — 16 `set_badge` steps ran before
   `manage_novel (action: create)`, relying on the false-success behaviour this
   increment removes. Swapped create-then-badge; blocking failures went 11 → 0.
   This is the harness proving F-P1-13 was real, not a test regression.
4. **Holosuite anchor drift** — `src/index.ts` shifted by ~58 lines and
   `state.ts` by 9. Repointed 18 anchors across `holosuite-{adversarial,
   conformance, invariants, novel,differential}.ts`. One repoint (`share`) was
   wrong twice: the first attempt hit `shareLicenseRefusal` (no `\b` token
   boundary), corrected to the `args.share === true` line.
5. **Three non-blocking pattern-buffer sub-workflows fail (S24, S35, S36).**
   Reproduced at HEAD with the increment's source changes reverted, so they are
   pre-existing, not caused by this work. Not fixed (out of scope; non-blocking).
   Recorded here as a P2 candidate; they need their own triage.
6. **C-2 capture cost** — measured 0.02 ms at 3.7 KB and **6.75 ms at 3.6 MB**,
   over the plan's 5 ms fallback. Instead of narrowing fidelity, the capture is
   now taken only when the log is within `AUDIT_APPEND_HEADROOM` (3) slots of a
   configured cap, so the normal and uncapped paths pay nothing and the expensive
   path runs only where an abort is possible. Amends C-2; recorded here.
