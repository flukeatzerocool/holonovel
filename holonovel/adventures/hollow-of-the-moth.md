# Hollow of the Moth

_A short investigative adventure for a small party._

## Overview — *Keeper only*

A mill town's lanterns have gone dark, and the moth cult that tends them wants
the party to believe the dark is a mercy.

## Adventure Hook

The miller's daughter has not come home, and the lanterns went out at dusk.

## Preview

A dark mill town, a patient cult, and a daughter who chose the dark.

## Scenes

### The Dark Mill

The mill wheel turns without water, and no one will say who keeps it turning.

### Lantern Row

Every lantern on the row is cold, but the wicks are wet.

### The Moth Shrine

A low stone shrine under the hill, its door furred with pale wings.

## NPCs

**Moth Keeper**
4 HP. A soft-spoken cultist who offers the party a place in the dark.

**Miller Anseth**
A grieving father who hides a lantern under his floorboards.

## Factions

**Lantern Council** — the town council controls the oil ration and profits when
the lanterns stay lit.
