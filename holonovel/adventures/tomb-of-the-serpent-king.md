# Tomb of the Serpent King

_A dungeon adventure for 4–6 delvers._

## Overview — *Keeper only*

The tomb lies beneath the Marsh of Whispers. The Serpent King's crown was
sealed here three centuries ago, and the marsh has been rising ever since.

## Adventure Hook

The village elder offers 500 gold for the Crown.

## Preview

A drowned tomb, a covetous elder, and a crown that does not want to leave.

## World

The Entrance Chamber is a room. "A dusty room with faded murals of serpent figures."
North of the Entrance Chamber is the Hall of Statues.
The Hall of Statues is a room. "Tall statues line both walls."
North of the Hall of Statues is the Inner Sanctum.
The Inner Sanctum is a room. "A chamber lit by an eerie green glow."

## Scenes

### Entrance Chamber

Murals of serpent figures coil across the walls, their eyes worn smooth by
centuries of pilgrims.

### Hall of Statues

Tall statues line both walls, each holding a stone blade pointed at the door.

### Inner Sanctum

A chamber lit by an eerie green glow. The crown rests on a stone dais.

## NPCs

**Serpent King Ghost**
3 HP. A crowned shade that demands the intruders name their debts.

**Marsh Guide**
A nervous local who knows the safe paths through the reeds.

## Factions

**Marshwardens Guild** — the guild taxes every relic recovered from the marsh
and keeps a ledger of debts.

## Encounters

@encounter(Entrance Chamber) Poison dart trap.
