## Module: supplementary_guidance (5 items)

### `lgm-eight-steps` — HIGH
The Lazy GM eight-step session prep checklist: (1) review the characters, (2) create a
strong start, (3) outline potential scenes, (4) define secrets and clues, (5) develop
fantastic locations, (6) outline important NPCs, (7) choose relevant monsters, (8) select
magic item rewards. This maps to Holonovel's Novel setup: review character sheets, set
scene state, seed lore entries, create NPCs, load adventure content.

### `lgm-secrets-and-clues` — HIGH
Prepare ten secrets and clues per session. Secrets are facts the characters can discover;
they advance the story regardless of how they're found. Drop clues wherever the
characters look — the clue finds the PCs, not the other way around. Maps to Holonovel's
lore entry system (REQ-083) and secret system (REQ-234).

### `lgm-node-based-encounters` — HIGH
Treat locations as nodes, not linear paths. Each node contains: area description, notable
NPCs, potential encounters, and clues that lead to other nodes. The GM improvises the
connections based on player choices. Maps to Holonovel's adventure structure (REQ-079)
and world-model room system.

### `lgm-improv-dials` — MEDIUM
Three improvisation dials for any scene: (1) what does the environment look/sound/smell
like, (2) what danger or opportunity is present, (3) what clue or revelation might the
characters discover. Adjust each dial to control pacing: crank danger for tension, crank
clues for discovery, crank environment for atmosphere.

### `lgm-player-spotlight` — HIGH
Rotate spotlight among players: frame-scene for each character, ask what they're doing,
resolve actions, move to next. In solo play, this maps to multi-character POV switching
per REQ-220. Ensure each entity gets narrative focus proportionally.

## Module: adventure_advice (4 items)

### `lgm-five-room-dungeon` — HIGH
Category: adventure_templates
Content: Five-room dungeon structure: (1) entrance with guardian, (2) puzzle or
roleplaying challenge, (3) trick or setback, (4) climax or boss encounter, (5) reward or
revelation. Adaptable to non-dungeon settings by treating "rooms" as narrative beats.

### `lgm-strong-start` — HIGH
Category: adventure_templates
Content: Every session begins with a strong start: a moment of immediate action, a
dramatic question, or a sensory-rich description that pulls players into the fiction.
Skip administrative recaps until after the hook lands. Maps to `set_scene_state` with
`fast_forward` per REQ-252.

### `lgm-flexible-encounters` — HIGH
Category: table_expansions
Content: Encounter templates by tier: low (1d4 weak monsters + environmental hazard),
medium (1 tough monster + 2 minions + terrain feature), high (boss monster + lair action
+ minion waves). Templates accept any monster stat block — populate from the ruleset's
bestiary.

### `lgm-reward-pacing` — MEDIUM
Category: scenario_starters
Content: Magic item distribution rhythm: one consumable per encounter, one permanent item
per two sessions, one signature item per tier. Rewards tied to character backstory goals
create stronger engagement than random treasure.

## Module: briefing_order (1 item)

### `lgm-session-flow` — HIGH
Recommended briefing section ordering for session flow: scene_state → entities →
narrative_threads → available_actions → combat_state → lore → adventure → narrative_directive
→ foundations → anti_slop. This ordering surfaces immediate decision-critical context
first, followed by guidance and reference material.
